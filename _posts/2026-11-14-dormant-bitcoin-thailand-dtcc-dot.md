{% capture t %}**Thailand's Securities and Exchange Commission** proposed on 12 November to cap stablecoin deposits and withdrawals at regulated crypto platforms at 5 million baht (about $148,000) per customer per day, counted separately for each direction and each platform [4][5][6]. Customers could only move stablecoins to and from accounts or wallets verified as their own, and transfers to or from other people's wallets would be banned [4][5]. The regulator says the aim is to curb money laundering, cybercrime and ways around international money-transfer rules; comments close on 26 November [4].{% endcapture %}
