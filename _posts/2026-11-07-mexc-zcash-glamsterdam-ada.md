{% capture t %}**MEXC** is being cut off from the US financial system: on Friday, November 6, the US Treasury's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) issued an order that bars US financial institutions from processing funds transfers to or from MEXC Global [1][2]. Treasury says the exchange facilitated at least $16 billion in illicit transactions between May 2021 and May 2026, linked to Iranian, North Korean and Russian sanctions evasion and to cybercrime [1][2]. These are the agency's allegations; the order takes effect 30 days after it is published in the Federal Register [1][2].{% endcapture %}

{% capture t %}**Zcash** activated its NU7 network upgrade at block 3,543,405, mined at 02:34 UTC on November 6 [3][4]. The target time between blocks drops from 75 to 25 seconds, and block rewards are scaled down by the same factor, so new-coin issuance stays about the same [4]. A new mechanism also moves 60% of each block's fees out of circulation into a public reserve, and the roughly 12,224 ZEC left in the old Sprout pool can no longer be spent [4].{% endcapture %}

{% capture t %}**Ethereum** developers are keeping the Glamsterdam upgrade off the Hoodi test network until risks to existing smart contracts are resolved [5][6]. Two proposals in the upgrade, EIP-8037 and EIP-8038, change how gas (the fee for computation and storage) is charged and can break eight assumptions some contracts rely on, the Ethereum Foundation wrote on November 6 [5][6]. The post asks contract developers and auditors to check their projects and gives no new date for the test fork [5].{% endcapture %}

Intersect, Cardano's member organisation, calls it a step for node diversity and notes that Amaru's minimum viable block producer milestone is still open, with a November 12 target [8]. On Kraken, ADA closed at about $0.184 on Friday, November 6, after a three-month stretch that opened near $0.20 on August 7 and peaked at about $0.28 on October 6 [9].{% endcapture %}
