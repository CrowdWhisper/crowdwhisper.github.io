**The idea.** "Trading crypto" covers everything from buying a coin once to running 50x leverage on a phone, and each style fails in its own way. Below are twelve, each with pros, cons and a risk score from 1 to 5. The score is our own judgement, not a measurement: how much of your stake you could realistically lose, and how fast, price swings included.

{% capture t %}**Buy the coin and hold it**, either all at once or in fixed amounts on a schedule (dollar-cost averaging, DCA).

- **Pros:** simple; no liquidation, so a drop alone cannot force you out; DCA removes the urge to time the market.
- **Cons:** full price swings: bitcoin fell about 78% from its November 2021 high of about $69,000 to its November 2022 low of about $15,500 [1]; coins left on an exchange depend on that exchange.
- **Risk 3/5.** Main failure: a deep drawdown, or selling in panic at the bottom.{% endcapture %}

{% capture t %}**Day trading**: opening and closing positions within a day to catch small moves.

- **Pros:** no overnight exposure; quick feedback; works in rising and falling markets.
- **Cons:** fees and spreads add up on every trade; you compete with professionals and algorithms. Of 1,551 people who started day trading Brazil's equity-index futures in 2013–15 and kept going for more than 300 days, 97% lost money, with no sign of improving with practice [2]. Not crypto, but similar mechanics.
- **Risk 4/5.** Main failure: death by a thousand fees and overtrading.{% endcapture %}
{% include news-item.html avatar="fact-bolt.png" alt="Lightning bolt" text=t %}

{% capture t %}**Swing trading**: holding a position for days or weeks to catch a larger move.

- **Pros:** fewer trades and fees; time to think; no leverage needed.
- **Cons:** news can gap the price against you overnight; a "swing" easily becomes an unplanned long hold.
- **Risk 3/5** without leverage. Main failure: no exit plan, or a stop-loss that is never set.{% endcapture %}
{% include news-item.html avatar="fact-wave.png" alt="Wave" text=t %}

{% capture t %}**Perpetual futures**: contracts that track a coin's price with no expiry date, usually traded with borrowed money (leverage).

- **Pros:** shorting a fall is as easy as buying a rise; little capital needed; can hedge a holding.
- **Cons:** liquidation. A 10x long is closed out after a drop of about 9.5%, a 50x long after about 1.5% (chart; an exchange's isolated-margin formula, 0.5% maintenance margin as an example) [3]. In the 90 days to 10 November, bitcoin dipped 1.5% or more below its daily open on about 36% of days [1]. A "funding rate" paid between longs and shorts adds cost; when positive, longs pay [4][5]. EU regulators found 74–89% of retail accounts lost money in a similar leveraged product (CFDs) and in 2018 agreed to cap retail crypto CFD leverage at 2:1 [6].
- **Risk 5/5.** Main failure: one normal wick wipes the margin.{% endcapture %}

{% capture t %}**Options**: the right, not the duty, to buy (call) or sell (put) at a set price before a set date.

- **Pros:** a buyer can lose at most the premium; puts can insure a holding.
- **Cons:** the premium is lost if the option expires worthless; sellers (writers) of some options face potentially unlimited losses [7]; fair prices are hard to judge.
- **Risk 4/5** buying, **5/5** selling uncovered. Main failure: time runs out before the move comes.{% endcapture %}
{% include news-item.html avatar="fact-ticket.png" alt="Ticket" text=t %}

{% capture t %}**Staking**: locking coins to help secure a proof-of-stake network for rewards. Native: you or a provider run a validator. Liquid: a pool gives you a token representing your stake.

- **Pros:** rewards come from the protocol; for ether they are modest: about 2.5% a year on ethereum.org and about 2.3% (seven-day average) on Lido when checked on 11 November [8][9]; liquid tokens stay tradeable.
- **Cons:** full price risk remains; offline validators lose small amounts and misbehaving ones are "slashed" [8]; exits can queue; liquid staking adds contract and operator risk [8].
- **Risk 2.5/5** native, **3/5** liquid. Main failure: a price fall that dwarfs the yield, or a pool or contract failure.{% endcapture %}
{% include news-item.html avatar="fact-seedling.png" alt="Seedling" text=t %}

{% capture t %}**Lending and borrowing**: supplying coins to a lending protocol for interest, or borrowing against coins you deposit.

- **Pros:** interest on idle coins; borrowing lets you raise cash without selling.
- **Cons:** borrowers are liquidated when collateral falls too far; on Aave, once the "health factor" drops below 1, a liquidator can repay up to half, or all, of the debt and take that value plus a bonus from your collateral [10]; lenders carry contract risk and can be stuck if a pool runs dry.
- **Risk 3/5** supplying, **4/5** borrowing. Main failure: a fast price drop liquidates the loan.{% endcapture %}
{% include news-item.html avatar="fact-handshake.png" alt="Handshake" text=t %}

{% capture t %}**Earn accounts**: a company (centralised finance, CeFi) takes your coins, lends them on and pays you a rate.

- **Pros:** one click; no wallets or contracts to manage.
- **Cons:** you are a creditor of a company you cannot audit. Celsius froze withdrawals on 12 June 2022 and filed for bankruptcy a month later [11][12]; the CFTC says its liabilities then exceeded its assets by more than $1 billion [11]. BlockFi followed in November 2022, citing FTX exposure [13].
- **Risk 4/5.** Main failure: the platform fails and withdrawals stop.{% endcapture %}
{% include news-item.html avatar="fact-compass.png" alt="Compass" text=t %}

{% capture t %}**Liquidity providing (LP)**: depositing two tokens into a trading pool and earning a share of the trading fees.

- **Pros:** fee income from other people's trades; works best when prices go back and forth.
- **Cons:** "impermanent loss": when one token moves against the other, you end up behind simply holding both. In a 50/50 pool a 2x move costs about 5.7% and a 5x move about 25.5%, before fees [14]; plus contract risk.
- **Risk 4/5** for volatile pairs. Main failure: a big one-way move outruns the fees.{% endcapture %}

{% capture t %}**Prediction markets**: trading yes/no shares on real-world events.

- **Pros:** capped stakes; prices read like probabilities; you can sell early. On Polymarket, shares trade between $0 and $1 and the correct outcome pays $1 [15].
- **Cons:** the wrong side goes to zero; outcomes depend on how each market's rules define and settle the event; access is restricted: Polymarket's own documentation lists dozens of countries and regions, including Germany, France, Italy, the Netherlands and the UK, where opening new positions is blocked (in some, only on its website) [16]. Check your local rules before using any prediction market.
- **Risk 4/5.** Main failure: being confidently wrong, often.{% endcapture %}
{% include news-item.html avatar="fact-crystal-ball.png" alt="Crystal ball" text=t %}

{% capture t %}**Copy trading and bots**: automatically mirroring another trader, or letting software trade a set strategy.

- **Pros:** saves time; rules are applied without emotion.
- **Cons:** past results get more space than risks; lead traders' incentives can differ from yours, and EU regulator ESMA asks supervisors to check how they are paid, for example by number of followers or trades copied [17]; a bot follows its rules into markets it was never tested on.
- **Risk 4/5.** Main failure: copying someone's leverage without knowing it.{% endcapture %}
{% include news-item.html avatar="fact-robot.png" alt="Robot" text=t %}

{% capture t %}**Airdrop farming**: using new protocols early in the hope of a free token distribution.

- **Pros:** little capital needed; you learn new tools hands-on.
- **Cons:** fees and hours for an uncertain reward; projects screen out farms: in 2024 LayerZero offered self-reporting farmers 15% of their allocation and nothing to those it caught [18]; fake "claim" sites drain wallets.
- **Risk 2.5/5** for money, higher for your wallet's safety. Main failure: signing something on a fake claim page.{% endcapture %}
{% include news-item.html avatar="fact-parachute.png" alt="Parachute" text=t %}

{% capture t %}The more borrowed money, counterparty trust or time pressure a style needs, the higher it sits. Whatever the style, size positions so being wrong is affordable, and decide your exit before you enter.{% endcapture %}
