{% capture t %}**MSCI**, the index provider, will remove Strategy, the largest listed company holding bitcoin, from its global equity indexes, including the MSCI USA Index, as of the close on 30 November [1][2][3]. The cut follows MSCI's new rule for "non-operating" companies, which first checks whether a company has substantial operating assets and then tests it on five financial ratios [1]. Tokyo-listed bitcoin buyer Metaplanet was also excluded, according to Reuters and CoinDesk [2][3]. The decision does not delist Strategy's shares or force it to sell bitcoin, but funds that copy these indexes will have to sell the stock [3].{% endcapture %}
