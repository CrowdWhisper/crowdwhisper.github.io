{% capture t %}**China**'s internet regulator and ten other government bodies, including the central bank and the public security ministry, published rules on 27 November for digital identities built on blockchain technology, which take effect on 1 March 2027 [3][4][5]. The identities run on the country's state-planned blockchain network, registration is voluntary, and users can choose to share only selected details of a credential, for example to prove a qualification [4][5]. Identity providers may not use verification activity to build user profiles or target advertising, and must get consent before linking an identity to a user's other accounts [4][5].{% endcapture %}
