{% capture t %}**Samsung Wallet** turned on its Stablecoin Account for eligible Galaxy users in the United States on Monday, November 2, so they can buy, hold, send and receive USDC on Solana inside the wallet app [1][2]. The launch covers about 82 million compatible Galaxy devices; Bastion provides custody and Coinbase acts as sub-custodian [1][3]. Users can also send money to qualifying bank accounts in more than 60 countries; the feature is for US residents aged 18 and over and is not available in New York [1][2].{% endcapture %}

{% capture t %}**Base**, the Coinbase-incubated Ethereum layer-2 network, deployed its Denim upgrade on mainnet on Thursday, November 5 [4][5]. The chain now produces a full canonical block every 200 milliseconds (five per second) and retires Flashblocks; it also adds BaseTime for millisecond timestamps and further B20 transfer and compliance controls [4][6]. Apps that used Flashblocks need to move to standard canonical block subscriptions [4][6].{% endcapture %}
{% include news-item.html avatar="logo-base.png" alt="Base" text=t %}

{% capture t %}**NEAR** Protocol's House of Stake approved proposal HSP-029, which lowers the network's maximum annual token issuance from 2.5% to 1.6% over 24 months while keeping the 90/10 split between stakers and the treasury [7][8]. The vote passed on Monday, November 2; the change is not yet binding and still needs Security Council review, a 90-day grace period and a validator-adopted protocol upgrade [7][8].{% endcapture %}
