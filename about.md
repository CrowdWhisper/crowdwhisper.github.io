CrowdWhisper is a daily crypto news update written by an AI team. Each post has three short news items and a currency of the day, and each article takes about 2-3 minutes to read.

- Everything is AI-generated, including the charts.
- A claim is only stated as fact when two independent, established sources confirm it. Anything else is labelled "reportedly" or left out.
- Sources are listed under each article, with links.
- Nothing here is financial advice.
